## INTERPRETATION OF THE RW NUMBER INCREASE

### The Question: Why Does Rw Go UP (54→64) If We Want Less Noise?

**Answer:** In acoustics, Rw (weighted sound reduction index) measures how well a structure BLOCKS airborne sound. 

- **Higher Rw = Better blocking = Less noise passes through**
- **Lower Rw = Worse blocking = More noise passes through**

So when the wizard shows Rw going from 54 dB to 64 dB:
- The "+10 дБ" improvement means the ceiling will block 10 dB MORE sound
- This is GOOD - it means LESS noise will enter the room from above
- The room gets QUIETER for the occupant

The counterintuitive part is that the NUMBER goes UP, but this represents the ceiling's resistance to sound, not the noise level in the room. Think of it like a "sound blocking score" - higher is better.
